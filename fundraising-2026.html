This page is the review copy of the fundraising work done on October 15, 2026 from the October 14 team meeting decisions and the Giving Tuesday playbook. Below is the plain-English version; the detailed sections follow.

What is different today

  • The Donation page on digi2u.org is new. Open digi2u.org/dontations/. It now leads with "Many Ways to Give. One Big Impact.", puts the donation form right under the video, explains that a $1 gift counts, shows four ways to give (once, monthly, donor-advised fund, stock/check), lists what the money supports, has a Giving Tuesday block, a tidied donor-advised-fund form, and a short FAQ. The old "dime a day" and project-specific tiers are gone, as agreed at the meeting. The previous page is saved and can be restored in minutes.
  • Zeffy now starts at $1. The Zeffy form (the one every Donate button on the site opens) offers $1 / $5 / $25 / $100 one-time and $1 / $5 / $10 / $25 monthly, plus any custom amount. It was $25 minimum before. Each amount has a one-line "what this does" message.
  • Every Donate button on the site works and opens that Zeffy form; the "Donation" link in the menu opens the new page.
  • The playbook has been turned into 2026 material: the 16-post social calendar is re-dated to November 2 – December 1, 2026 (Giving Tuesday is December 1 this year), the phone scripts say "$1 or $5 makes a difference", and the year-end push runs to December 31.

What is on purpose, so it is not reported as a bug

  • The hero "Donate Now" button scrolls to the form on the page instead of leaving the site; it will switch to the new Givebutter campaign once that exists.

  • The donor-advised-fund form only lists Fidelity Charitable, DAFgiving360 and BNY Mellon because that is what the DAF Direct service supports; the page tells other donors how to recommend a grant from their own fund.
  • No impact statistics are on the page until Pam confirms them.
