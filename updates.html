A running summary of what has been done to the live site for the team. Every item below is published and can be checked from the links provided.

This update covers the work completed today, plus the other changes shipped earlier this week. The full site map is at the bottom.

Shipped today — September 30

✓ Donate button added to every hero

A “Donate Now” button now appears in the hero of every page across the site — 32 pages, matching the homepage. It links straight to the donation page, so supporters can give from anywhere on the site.

See it live: Marketing 3D Design About Events

✓ Program pages made consistent

Confirmed every page opens with a video header. The 12 program pages were normalized to one consistent layout, and the Health & Wellness page was cleaned up (empty gaps removed, headings unified with the rest).

Examples: Health & Wellness Drone All Programs

✓ Content reorganized

The “Digi2U Systems” section was moved onto the About page. The Partnerships block — partner cards plus the Community Partners and Corporate Supporters carousels — was moved onto the Community Partners page, where it fits better.

About Community Partners

✓ Faster page loads

Removed two redundant fonts that were loading on every page: a 454 KB icon font used for a single mail icon (switched to a font already loaded), and a duplicate 58 KB FontAwesome set that the menu was loading on top of the theme’s. No visual change — pages simply load lighter, which helps most on mobile.
